Logos & Brand Mark

The Lucide Sprout icon is Nabta's primary mark — growth, learning, the plant meaning of نَبْتَة.

Primary Mark

The official mark is the Lucide Sprout icon (ISC license). Do not invent other logo artwork. Stroke uses currentColor; on brand surfaces that is Nabta accent #10B981 (HeroUI --accent).

Nabta نَبْتَة
Learn. Grow. Thrive.
On dark — sprout inherits currentColor

Source file: docs/assets/sprout.svg. Inline the same SVG in the docs top nav, sidebar, mobile bar, and hub hero.

Wordmark

  • Latin: Nabta — IBM Plex Sans Semibold
  • Arabic: نَبْتَة — IBM Plex Sans Arabic
  • Tagline: Learn. Grow. Thrive.

Usage

  • Docs site: sprout + wordmark in top nav, sidebar, and mobile bar
  • Portals: sprout in app shell header (when logo assets are wired in Phase 1)
  • Marketing site: sprout + full wordmark on home page (Phase 1b)

Do Not

  • Stretch or rotate the sprout icon
  • Use colors other than Nabta accent (#10B981 / --accent) on the mark (except white on dark backgrounds)
  • Replace with a different icon without updating this spec
