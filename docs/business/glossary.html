Glossary

Domain terms used across Nabta docs. Read this before implementing to avoid ambiguous naming.

Tip for AI: When a term has two meanings (e.g. grade), always use the full entity name in code and APIs — GradeLevel vs GradeRecord.

Organization

TermDefinition
SchoolA tenant. All data is scoped by schoolId. Unique URL-safe slug and default locale. One Nabta deployment serves many schools.
Academic YearA school year (e.g. 2026/2027). Contains configurable terms (typically 2–4). Grade levels and classes belong to the year, not to a term.
TermA calendar/reporting slice of an academic year — e.g. Term 1, Term 2, Term 3. Egyptian international schools usually have three terms; MoE national private schools have two. Classes are not nested under terms. Entity: Term.
Grade LevelA year group — e.g. Year 7, Grade 10, KG2. Not a score. Entity: Grade or GradeLevel.
ClassA homeroom or teaching group within a grade level for the whole academic year — e.g. "Year 7A". Students belong to classes via enrollments.
SubjectAn academic discipline — e.g. Mathematics, Physics. Taught to a class or group of students.
EnrollmentLinks a student to a class (and optionally subjects) for an academic year.

Academic Work

TermDefinition
LessonA unit of content within a subject — may include materials, videos, readings.
MaterialA file or resource attached to a lesson (PDF, video, link).
AssignmentWork students must complete and submit by a due date.
SubmissionA student's response to an assignment.
AssessmentA quiz or test — may be auto-graded or manually graded.
Grade RecordA score or mark for an assignment, assessment, or term. Not a grade level. Entity: GradeRecord or Mark.
AttendanceRecord of student presence per class/session.
AnnouncementA message from teacher or admin to students — class, subject, grade, or school-wide.

Users & Roles

TermDefinition
UserAuth identity (email, password). Has one role per school: STUDENT, TEACHER, or ADMIN.
StudentUser with role STUDENT. Sees student portal.
TeacherUser with role TEACHER. Manages classes, content, grading.
AdminUser with role ADMIN. Manages one school tenant — structure, users, settings. Uses the school admin portal in apps/web.
System AdminUser with role SYSTEM_ADMIN. Nabta platform operator — manages schools, health, and ops across tenants. Uses apps/system-admin; does not manage school academic features.
PortalRole-specific web experience in apps/web — student, teacher, or school admin. Distinct from mobile apps and the system-admin console.
AppNative mobile client — apps/mobile-student (students) or apps/mobile-staff (teachers + school admins). Expo + HeroUI Native.

Platform

TermDefinition
Nabta (نَبْتَة)The product name. Tagline: Learn. Grow. Thrive.
PlatformThe Nabta multi-tenant backend and operator layer — all schools share one API; SYSTEM_ADMIN manages the platform, not individual school academics.
PhaseAn incremental implementation slice. Always implement one phase at a time.
Marketing SitePublic website at apps/marketing — prospective schools; not a portal. Phase 1b.
Mobile StudentNative student app at apps/mobile-student. Phase 1d.
Mobile StaffNative app for teachers and school admins at apps/mobile-staff. Phase 1e.
Portal vs AppPortal = web SPA in the browser (apps/web role routes). App = installed Expo client on iOS/Android. Both call the same /api/v1 API.
