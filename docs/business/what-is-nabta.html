What is Nabta?

Nabta (نَبْتَة) is a school platform built for the Middle East — starting with Egyptian international schools — that works like a modern Moodle, but purpose-built for how schools actually operate.

The Problem with Generic LMS

Moodle and similar platforms were designed around courses and activities. International schools operate around schools, academic years, grade levels, classes, and subjects. Fitting a school into a course-centric model creates friction for admins, teachers, and students.

What Nabta Is

Nabta sits between:

  • LMS — lessons, materials, assignments, assessments
  • Student Information System — enrollments, academic structure
  • Digital classroom — day-to-day teaching and learning workflows
  • Assessment platform — quizzes, grading, progress tracking

Three primary portals — Student, Teacher, and Admin — each answering a single daily question.

Nabta vs Moodle

DimensionMoodleNabta
Core modelCourses + activitiesSchool → year → grade → class → subject
Target userUniversities, generic online learningEgyptian international schools (MENA growth path)
CustomizationPlugin marketplace, heavy configurationOpinionated product; curriculum as config, not plugins
LanguagesMany locales; RTL often bolted onEN/AR + RTL from Phase 1
UXFunctional but dated; admin-heavyModern SaaS — calm, fast, role-based
Multi-tenancySite/course hierarchySchool = tenant; all data scoped by schoolId

Regional Focus

Launch market: Egyptian international schools running British, American, or IB-style structures. Curriculum specifics are configurable — not hardcoded — so Nabta can expand across MENA international schools without architectural rewrites.

Non-Goals

  • Not a MOOC or online-course marketplace
  • Not a plugin ecosystem clone of Moodle
  • Not a gamified learning app
  • Not an old-school ERP with dense interfaces
